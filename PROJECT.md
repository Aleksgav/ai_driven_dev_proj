# A web application for task estimation: Scrum Planning Poker

The team meets in application and estimates every task for next sprint
In application, the team has cards for estimation and list of tasks.
Tasks are fetched from Jira (or another task management application).
Cards display values such as Fibonacci, Modified Fibonacci, or other types of values.
For each vote, every team member votes secretly; after voting, the cards are flipped to reveal values.
After voting, the average, maximum, and minimum values are calculated and saved to history.
